# Indie Hacking

This is my research hub for turning ideas into products independently—from
finding an idea and building it to launching it and keeping it alive.

I use **indie hacking** as a loose umbrella for solo development, solo founding,
bootstrapping, and everything around them.

## Research

- [App Review and paywall design](app-review/README.md): current Apple guidance,
  developer rejection and approval cases, fourteen illustrated paywall cases
  from social posts, developer blogs, and newsletters,
  and a design/submission checklist.
